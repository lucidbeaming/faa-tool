## 2026 Microsoft outage

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Microsoft_outage (read 2026-11-14)

- Started November 12, 2026, around 17:00-18:00 UTC: one day before the FAA ground stop. Still recovering on November 13.
- Hit Microsoft 365, Outlook email (Exchange Online), Teams and Copilot. Users in more than 70 countries reported problems; about 140,000 reports on Downdetector by the evening of November 12.
- Microsoft's explanation: settings applied to servers after an update overwhelmed the process that sets up secure connections, so servers stopped answering. Not a cyberattack, not AI agents. Microsoft rolled back the network configuration change.
- Knock-on effects: government offices in several countries; US House staff lost a day of work; traders went back to phones and fax machines; UK hospitals cancelled some operations; a Canadian health network declared a "code grey"; airlines reported problems.
- Microsoft said it was "deeply sorry."

## Claude (Anthropic) outage

Source: Claude status page, November 13, 2026

- November 13, 2026, the same day as the FAA ground stop: a major outage lasting 4 hours 37 minutes, plus 57 minutes of partial outage.
- Elevated errors on claude.ai, the Claude API, Claude Code and platform.claude.com, and on the Microsoft 365 connector.
